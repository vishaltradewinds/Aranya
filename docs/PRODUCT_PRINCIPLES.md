# ARANYA Product Principles

1. **Simple and pure work. No nonsense.**
2. **Enter ARANYA, don't learn an application.** The user speaks, explores and follows the next useful step.
3. **ARANYA is the digital jungle.** The digital experience represents the real ecosystem and always connects back to real-world action.
4. **Walk the real work.** A journey is the primary user experience; the app should stay out of the way while work happens.
5. **AI is invisible intelligence.** AI understands language, images, context and intent; it does not manufacture authority.
6. **One common network; stakeholder-specific paths.** Every stakeholder enters the same ARANYA, while legitimate role, authority, context and task determine the path.
7. **Network-to-Network is a first-class primitive.** Onboard networks and connect them; do not depend on one-by-one national registration.
8. **Evidence before claims.** Claimed, evidenced, verified, accepted, payable and paid are distinct states.
9. **Real work creates the record.** Capture evidence naturally during execution rather than creating duplicate reporting work.
10. **ARANYA facilitates; it does not replace statutory authorities.** Human authority remains human.
11. **No silent changes to historical records.** Provenance and auditability are mandatory.
12. **Rules are jurisdiction- and product-aware.** Applicable requirements must remain configurable and versioned.
13. **Voice, local language, assisted and offline workflows are first-class.** The stakeholder adapts as little as possible to the system.
14. **A registration is not successful onboarding.** Activation, connection, completed work, transaction, settlement and repeat use matter more.
15. **Do not brand participation around pricing tiers.** The experience should focus on work and outcomes; commercial services can sit underneath.
16. **Every feature must pass the real-work test:** does it help a legitimate stakeholder discover, decide, connect, act, verify, transact, learn or create an outcome?
17. **Explore before freezing.** Do not prematurely lock the full ecosystem taxonomy, module structure or national rollout architecture.
