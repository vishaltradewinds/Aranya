# ARANYA Product Principles

1. Easy for people. Rigorous underneath.
2. ARANYA facilitates; it does not replace statutory authorities.
3. One common digital network; stakeholder-specific views.
4. Evidence before claims.
5. No silent changes to historical records.
6. Rules are jurisdiction- and product-aware.
7. Human authority remains human.
8. Offline-friendly and assisted workflows are first-class.
9. A registration is not a successful onboarding; a completed real transaction is.
10. Build and test one complete physical journey before scaling.
